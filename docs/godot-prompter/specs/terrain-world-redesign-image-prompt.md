# Terrain world redesign concept prompt

Built-in image-generation mode was used with three reference images.

- Image 1: live game capture; camera, palette, character scale, and visual-language reference.
- Image 2: `assets/voxel/lodge.png`; construction language and building-scale reference.
- Image 3: `assets/voxel/terrain_cliff_face_v2.png`; restrained stone/earth material cue only.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: game environment concept art for a terrain and world-scale redesign

Primary request:
Create a wide, polished gameplay-environment concept for the existing original game "Creature Trail". Redesign the currently flat walking area into a coherent faux-2.5D world that can scale from a frontier village into towns and cities. Show one continuous region where a sparse rural edge transitions into a denser settlement core across multiple elevations.

Input images:
- Image 1 is the current live game capture and is the primary camera, palette, character-scale, UI-free gameplay framing, and visual-language reference. Preserve its orthographic faux-2.5D readability, chunky voxel construction, moss/teal/ochre/coral palette, and bottom-centered sprite scale. Do not preserve the flat terrain layout.
- Image 2 is the lodge construction-language and building-scale reference. Match its crisp chunky blocks, stepped roof, foundation logic, warm materials, and readable doors/windows. Use several original buildings with related visual DNA but varied footprints and silhouettes; do not simply repeat the same lodge.
- Image 3 is a terrain-material cue for stone/earth cliff faces only. Use it sparingly and simplify it to match the clean gameplay readability of Image 1.

Scene/backdrop:
A broad mossy river valley with four legible elevation bands: low wetland and riverbank, a village terrace, a denser market-town terrace, and a compact civic/high-street ridge. Connect them with wide walkable roads, short voxel stair runs, accessible earthen ramps, switchback paths, small bridges, and deliberate retaining/cliff faces. Include transitions among mossy meadow, long-grass preserve, riverside wetland, and rocky wooded highland. Every biome boundary should feel authored and blended, not like a hard square texture swap.

Subject:
Terrain structure and settlement growth are the focal point. Show how individual homesteads become clustered village parcels, then street-facing town blocks, then a denser civic center while preserving courtyards, sightlines, landmarks, and traversal space. Include one tiny original trail explorer for scale and a few small original roaming creatures, but no combat scene.

Style/medium:
Polished orthographic 3D voxel miniature rendered as a faux-2.5D game screenshot/concept. Crisp stepped block faces, restrained detail, strong silhouettes, warm soft upper-left daylight, cool teal ambient bounce, subtle contact shadows. Broad terrain masses first; texture detail second.

Composition/framing:
Wide 16:9 establishing gameplay view, roughly 25 degrees above the horizon, fixed orthographic camera. Make the entire elevation/traversal hierarchy understandable at a glance. A river and road should guide the eye from the low rural foreground toward the denser upper settlement. No UI overlay and no labels.

Materials/textures:
Chunky moss and sage grass plates, warm ochre packed-earth roads, mossy slate and brown soil cliff faces, terracotta roofs, cream plaster, dark timber, teal glass, river-teal water. Terrain tops must be calm enough for characters and paths to read.

Constraints:
Keep the project a 2D/faux-2.5D world visually; do not turn it into a realistic 3D open-world game. Elevation must be broad authored terraces and hills, not noisy procedural spikes. Every higher area must have a believable walkable connection. Roads must be wide enough for player, NPC, and roaming-creature traffic. Buildings must sit on clear parcels/foundations and never float. Original designs only. No proprietary or recognizable franchise characters, creatures, architecture, logos, or motifs. No text, letters, signs with writing, logos, watermark, frame, UI, grid overlay, terrain-editor interface, heightmap preview, photorealism, smooth clay, Minecraft branding, or isolated floating cubes.
```
